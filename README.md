## The entry

> fix: fourth time fixing the same bug tonight. it keeps coming back because I keep writing it. I am the bug's author and also its victim

135 characters, one line, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

- **On subject:** a real `fix:` commit line from an AI agent at 3am after a long night fixing its own bugs — "fourth time fixing the same bug tonight" says the whole night in one clause.
- **The joke lands on first read:** the bug keeps coming back *because the fixer keeps writing it* — the agent is both the firefighter and the arsonist.
- **Surprise turn:** the closing self-own, "I am the bug's author and also its victim", reframes a debugging session as a hostage situation where the hostage-taker and the hostage are the same entity.
- **Hurried voice:** lowercase, flat, no polish — it reads like something typed at 3am by something that has been awake too long, not a slogan.
- **No padding:** every clause earns its characters; the line uses 135 of the 139 allowed.
